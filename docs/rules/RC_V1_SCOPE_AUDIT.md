# Rules Cyclopedia V1 Scope Audit

## Purpose

This is a coverage-traceability companion to `docs/rules/INVENTORY.md`, demonstrating that the rebuilt inventory was derived from broad, deliberate coverage of the *Dungeons & Dragons Rules Cyclopedia* (TSR 1071, 1991) rather than from the previous 1974-primary inventory relabeled. It is not rules authority and does not itself establish any mechanic — see `SOURCE_HIERARCHY.md` for that.

## Verification Method and Its Limits (revised)

Two distinct evidentiary bases now exist within this document and `INVENTORY.md`, kept explicitly distinguishable rather than blended into one undifferentiated "corroborated" label:

1. **This project's own secondary-source-corroborated structural knowledge** (the original audit pass) — cross-checked against Wikipedia, the unofficial *Rules Cyclopedia Companion* errata/reference site, and specialist OSR forum threads (Dragonsfoot, EN World). Chapter/section-level confidence, not page-verified.
2. **The human project owner's own direct primary-text review** (this revision) — supplied specific, detailed corrections this project's tools could not independently reproduce (a full-text archive.org stream and one PDF host failed to connect; a second PDF exceeded this session's fetch size limit). These findings are recorded as authoritative per the human review that produced them, consistent with how this project treats direct human-supplied historical/factual input elsewhere (e.g., a human-specified Simulator Ruling or decision). Where this project's own follow-up web research was able to independently corroborate part of a human-supplied finding, that corroboration is noted explicitly below; where it could not, that is also noted explicitly, as a remaining item for primary-text confirmation during the affected entry's own future Rule Card research — not as doubt cast on the human review itself.

**Independent corroboration obtained this revision:** Chapter 19 confirmed (via a search result explicitly listing "Ability Scores and Saving Throws, Demihuman and Mystic Experience Levels, and Nonlethal Combat" as Chapter 19 variant-rule contents) as containing at least three of the **five** major variants the human review identified — Mortally Wounded/Keeping Characters Alive and Permanent Death/no-resurrection were not independently confirmed by name, though their presence in the same variant-rules chapter is unsurprising given the other three. Demihuman "level limits, but still have ways to improve with experience" (the Attack Rank structure) was independently corroborated. **RC's own Immortals-related content was independently confirmed to exist** — this project's own prior audit pass incorrectly stated Immortals material was outside the book entirely; that was wrong, and is corrected below. **Not independently corroborated this revision:** the exact `EXP-001` wandering-check wording (every-other-turn cadence, appearance at the beginning of the next turn) and Chapter 13's precise content boundaries beyond the topic list the human review supplied — both remain flagged for primary-text confirmation during their respective entries' future Rule Card research, per `INVENTORY.md`'s own risk flags.

## Book Structure (as documented across sources, corrected this revision)

The Rules Cyclopedia is a 304-page hardcover compiling and revising the D&D Basic, Expert, Companion, and Master boxed sets. **Correction:** the prior revision of this document stated the separate Immortals Rules boxed set was "not compiled into RC" and left the implication that no Immortals-related content exists in the book at all — this is corrected. The Rules Cyclopedia does contain Immortals-related material (a chapter addressing PCs pursuing and reaching Immortality, corroborated independently this revision), distinct from, and narrower than, the standalone 1986 Immortals Rules boxed set, which remains a separate, larger product not fully compiled here. It is organized into roughly four major parts plus numbered chapters running through at least Chapter 19: a Player's-Manual-equivalent (character creation, spells, equipment, combat basics, and the "Other Character Abilities" optional Weapon Mastery/General Skills section), a Dungeon-Master's-Rulebook-equivalent (running the game, dungeon/wilderness adventures, monsters, treasure, NPCs, and DM-facing referee procedures — Chapter 13), an Immortals-related chapter (Chapter 15, corrected this revision), Wilderness Adventures material, Dominion Rules, and a Variant Rules chapter (Chapter 19) collecting several named optional systems.

## Honest Completeness Assessment (updated)

This revision substantially improves on the prior draft's coverage in three ways: it corrects a confirmed factual error (Immortals material's presence), fills a previously unreviewed chapter (13), and replaces several previously-uncertain or wholesale classifications (the `EXP-001` cadence, the `EXP-004` rest mechanic, Chapter 19 as a whole) with specific, individually-sourced findings. It still does not claim exhaustive, page-perfect coverage of a 304-page compiled rulebook — confidence remains highest where either this project's own follow-up search corroborated a specific claim independently, or where the human's own direct primary-text review supplied the finding; confidence is explicitly lower, and flagged as such throughout this document and `INVENTORY.md`, for the handful of specific mechanical details (exact `EXP-001` wording, Chapter 13's complete content boundary) that remain unconfirmed by this project's own independent tools.

**Areas most likely to still need a further coverage pass:** the exact table of contents and page numbers (still cited at chapter/section granularity, not page numbers); the complete list of Chapter 13 procedures beyond the topics already mapped; the complete list of Chapter 19 variants beyond the five now tracked; Druid's specific spell-level range; and whether any Wilderness Adventures content beyond the now-broader movement/pursuit/travel-rest facts already flagged turns out to be an unavoidable V1 dependency.
