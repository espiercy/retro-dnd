## Rules Cyclopedia Explicitly Establishes

1. **Starting money.** `3d6 × 10` gold pieces, a one-time sum, stated identically in Ch. 1 p. 8 and Ch. 4 p. 62.
2. **A free starting kit**, stated three times with differing detail. Ch. 4 p. 62 is the most specific: two or three sets of plain clothes, a pair of shoes, a belt, and a belt-pouch.
3. **Coin conversions:** `1 pp = 5 gp = 10 ep = 50 sp = 500 cp`.
4. **Encumbrance unit:** one coin (`cn`) weighs one-tenth of a pound.
5. **Complete catalogs** with per-item cost and `Enc (cn)`: Weapons, Ammunition, Nets, Armor, Adventuring Gear.
6. **Container capacities:** Backpack `400 cn`, Sack small `200 cn`, Sack large `600 cn`, Belt pouch `50 cn`, Quiver (filled, total) `10 cn`.
7. **Container encumbrance rule** (Adventuring Gear footnote `*`): the printed value is the **empty** encumbrance; when filled, encumbrance is the container **plus its contents**.
8. **Worn-versus-packed clothing** (footnote `**`): the printed value is the encumbrance **if packed**; *"If the clothes are worn, disregard the encumbrance."*
9. **Ammunition already included** (Weapons note `a`): a missile weapon's printed `Enc` includes its normal load — bow 20 arrows, crossbow 30 quarrels, sling 30 stones, blowgun 3 darts — with stated conversion rates for varying it.
10. **Size-derived cost and encumbrance** for nets (`1 sp` and `1 cn` per square foot) and whips (`1 gp` and `10 cn` per foot).
11. **Per-class equipment legality**, stated in the Ch. 2 class entries and, for some items, marked in Ch. 4's note codes.
12. **The unlisted-item procedure** (Ch. 13 p. 147): the DM decides cost, encumbrance and other characteristics.

## Rules Cyclopedia Leaves Undefined / Ambiguous

Narrowly, and only these:

1. **Belt pouch arithmetic.** RC prints empty `2 cn`, capacity `50 cn`, and a worked filled total of `55 cn`. `2 + 50 = 52`. All three are on the same page, visually verified. → **`SR-6`**.
2. **Magic-User dagger status.** Ch. 2 p. 19 makes the dagger the unconditional baseline weapon and names a separate optional list; Ch. 4's note `w` (*"Magic-users may use this weapon at the DM's discretion"*) is applied to **both dagger rows**, placing the dagger inside the discretionary set. → **`SR-7`**.
3. **"Clothes, plain" footnote marker.** Prints `20***` (the *quiver* footnote) where every other clothing row prints `**`. → recorded as a **typographic defect**; the `**` rule is applied. Not a Simulator Ruling — see §Open Questions 1.
4. **Racial-armour penalty magnitude.** RC states *"The DM **can** impose penalties on a character who wears the armor of a different race"*, giving an unquantified movement reduction as an example. → **Not a gap.** RC delegates by design; this card records it as a **DM-discretionary input** and quantifies nothing.

## Simulator Ruling

### `SR-6` — Belt pouch filled encumbrance is `52 cn`

**Ruling.** A fully loaded belt pouch weighs **`52 cn`**. The two independently stated values are preserved:

```text
belt pouch, empty      2 cn     [RC Explicit]
belt pouch, capacity  50 cn     [RC Explicit]
belt pouch, filled     52 cn    [SR-6: 2 + 50, per the stated container rule]
```

The RC worked total of **`55 cn` is treated as erroneous**.

**Rationale.** RC states both the empty weight and the capacity explicitly, and separately states the rule that a filled container's encumbrance is the container plus its contents. Their mechanical combination is `52`. An inconsistent *derived* total does not override two independently stated *inputs*.

**Reading rejected.** That `55 cn` governs and one component value is wrong — which would imply an empty weight of `5 cn`, equally available on the page. **Not taken.** The ruling prefers the stated inputs to the stated output.

**What this does not do.** It does not resolve the RC's internal inconsistency — the printed page still disagrees with itself — and it does not generalise. No other container's printed values are altered; every other capacity-bearing container's arithmetic is already self-consistent.

### `SR-7` — A Magic-User may use a dagger unconditionally

**Ruling.** A Magic-User may use a **dagger** unconditionally. The **expanded weapon list** — staff, blowgun, flaming oil, holy water, net, thrown rock, sling, whip — remains **optional**, available under the applicable DM / simulation-policy discretion. Chapter 4's note `w`, **insofar as it makes the dagger itself discretionary**, is treated as a compilation defect.

**Rationale.** RC Ch. 2 p. 19 states the dagger as the baseline and *then separately* identifies the expanded list as optional. BECMI lineage evidence supports the same structure across two structural units, and BECMI's equipment tables carry no magic-user note code at all.

**Reading rejected.** That Ch. 4's note `w` governs and the dagger is DM-discretionary. **Not taken** — but not dismissed as obviously wrong either: Ch. 4 is the operative equipment table, so preferring Ch. 2 required positive grounds, which Ch. 2's own two-tier phrasing and the BECMI corroboration supply.

**Scope.** `SR-7` reaches **only** the dagger's conditional status. It does **not** alter note `w`'s effect on the other eight weapons, which remain discretionary exactly as printed.

### 2. Starting money

```text
starting_gold = roll("3d6") x 10        gold pieces
```

Range `30`–`180` gp. Uses the project RNG abstraction (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §5).

### 3. Free starting kit

A newly created character owns, without purchase:

```text
two or three sets of plain clothes
a pair of shoes
a belt
a belt-pouch
```

**Encumbrance consequence.** Plain clothes and shoes are **worn**, so footnote `**` applies and their encumbrance is **disregarded**. The belt-pouch is carried; empty it is `2 cn`, filled `52 cn` (`SR-6`).

### 4. Coin conversions

```text
1 pp = 5 gp = 10 ep = 50 sp = 500 cp
1 cn = one-tenth of a pound
```

### 5. Purchase procedure

```text
1. Determine starting money (SS2).
2. Select items from the Chapter 4 catalogs.
3. Reject any item not legal for the character's class (SS7).
4. Reject any item the DM availability policy excludes.
5. Sum cost. If cost > money held, the selection is invalid;
   remove items until it is not.
6. Deduct cost from money held. The remainder is retained.
```

### A. What this card does **not** own

- Movement derived from encumbrance → **`CHAR-005`**. This card supplies `cn` values only.
- Weapon damage, ranges, two-handed initiative loss, net entanglement, blackjack effects → `COMBAT-002`/`003`/`006`/`007`.

- Magic-item acquisition, use and restriction → `TREAS-003`/`TREAS-004`.
- Light-source radius and burn duration → `EXP-006`. This card owns torch and lantern **cost and encumbrance** only.
- Suit Armor's surprise effects → `ENC-002`.

```text

vehicles (cart, wagon)           Ch. 4 pp. 70
water transportation / passage   Ch. 4 pp. 70-72
siege equipment                  Ch. 4 pp. 72-74
```

These are **inside RC Chapter 4** but **outside V1** by human decision. No transport responsibility currently exists in `docs/rules/INVENTORY.md`; assigning one is a governance question, not an action for this card.

### C. Chapter 10 high-level creation — `NOT V1-WIRED`

RC Ch. 10 Steps 5, 7 and 8 (pp. 129–131) supply a **different** equipment pathway: cash equal to **1% of XP**, explicitly *"not used for purchasing items"*; equipment **granted** rather than bought; an alternate cash method at DM-set prices; two magic-item methods; and the **only RC statement distinguishing equipment owned from equipment carried** (*"characters keep many common supplies in storage and don't carry them around on adventures"*).

```text
STATUS:  researched / preserved / NOT V1-WIRED
```

**Not absorbed into this card and not made executable by it.** Recorded because `CHAR-001` §5 already lands Step 2 of the same procedure, so the gap is concrete rather than hypothetical — a high-level character the project can already generate has no landed money or equipment rule. **That remains true after this card is approved**, by design.

## Open Questions

**None block approval.**

1. **"Clothes, plain" prints `***` where every other clothing row prints `**`.** The `***` footnote governs quivers and is meaningless for clothing. This card applies the `**` rule (worn → disregard). Recorded as a **typographic-defect reading rather than a Simulator Ruling**, because no mechanic turns on it: under either footnote a *worn* garment contributes nothing, and the packed value is printed on the row. **If a human reviewer prefers this be elevated to a ruling, say so at approval.**
2. **`CHAR-004` chapter scope** — whether mounts, vehicles, ships and siege equipment ever enter this card. **V1 excludes them**, so the card is complete without them. Governance question, not a rules question.
3. **Chapter 10 high-level equipment pathway** — `NOT V1-WIRED`. Preserved in §C. A high-level character generated by landed `CHAR-001` §5 still has no money or equipment rule, **by design**.
4. **No transport responsibility exists in the inventory.** Recorded so it is not mistaken for an omission in this card.

**Explicitly closed, recorded so they are not re-raised:** whether the belt-pouch discrepancy is inherited (**no** — the item has no BECMI ancestor); whether RC's note `w` has a lineage ancestor (**no**); whether a general equipment list exists anywhere in BECMI beyond Basic and Expert (**no**, across nine core source units).
