## 5. Highest-Priority Open Question — Answered at the Evidence Level

**Question:** Does RC's hit-point determination incorporate an ability-score-derived modifier, and if so, is the magnitude specified by the HP procedure itself or by the general ability-score-effects responsibility?

**Evidence-supported answer — both halves:**

1. **Yes, a modifier exists.** Constitution modifies hit points, stated in four independent RC locations, and named in the Abilities and Adjustments Table (p. 9) as an adjustment to "Hit Points per Experience Level."
2. **The magnitude is supplied by the general ability-score-effects material, not by the HP procedure.** RC's "Roll for Hit Points" step (p. 7) contains no value; it redirects the reader to the Bonuses and Penalties for Ability Scores Table (p. 9), which is the shared, all-abilities adjustment table sitting inside the material this project has assigned to `CHAR-007`. The Constitution write-up (p. 10) issues the same redirection.

**Scope qualification established at the same time:** the modifier applies only to rolled Hit Dice, never to the post-9th-level fixed gains, and never below a floor of 1 hit point per roll.

**Consequence for repository metadata — reported, not applied:** `INVENTORY.md` currently declares `CHAR-003`'s dependencies as `CHAR-002` only. On this evidence, `CHAR-003` **cannot** produce an authoritative hit-point value without the Constitution adjustment value owned by `CHAR-007`, so the current dependency row appears **incomplete**. This is exactly the possibility the cluster record preserved as an open question. Per the task's §14 and the cluster record's own instruction, **no metadata was changed** and no `CHAR-003 → CHAR-007` dependency was declared; the finding is escalated for human evidence review.

## 8. Falsification Pass

**Challenge 1 — "Constitution modifies hit points."**
*Sought:* whether this applies to every character, every class, every level, and every score range. *Searched:* `Constitution adjustment` (all hits), Ch. 2 class entries, Ch. 1 pp. 10 and 12, Ch. 10 p. 129.
*Found:* a real and consistently stated limit — the adjustment applies only to rolled Hit Dice and explicitly ceases from 10th level, restated in every class entry.
**Disposition: QUALIFIED.** True, but bounded by level and by roll-versus-fixed-gain, not a blanket modifier.

**Challenge 2 — "The Constitution modifier's magnitude is part of the HP rule."**
*Sought:* any value stated within the HP procedure itself. *Searched:* Ch. 1 p. 7 read in full; Ch. 1 p. 10 Constitution section read in full.
*Found:* neither location states a value; both redirect to the shared adjustment table on p. 9.
**Disposition: REJECTED.** The magnitude is not part of the HP rule — this is the finding that drives §5's dependency conclusion.

**Challenge 3 — "Hit points could be reduced to zero or below by a Constitution penalty."**
*Sought:* a floor rule. *Searched:* `hit points cannot be lowered`, Ch. 1 p. 10 in context.
*Found:* an explicit floor of at least 1 hit point per roll.
**Disposition: REJECTED (a floor exists).** Recorded as a mechanical constraint that a naive "roll + modifier" reading would miss.

**Challenge 4 — "Hit Dice are rolled at every level."**
*Sought:* an upper bound. *Searched:* `up to 9th level`, `pre-set number of hit points`, Ch. 2 entries, Ch. 10 p. 129.
*Found:* rolling stops after 9th level (8th for halfling); fixed gains follow.
**Disposition: REJECTED as stated; replaced by the bounded finding.**

**Challenge 5 — "The per-class Hit Die values in the Ch. 1 table are complete and consistent with Chapter 2."**
*Sought:* disagreement between the p. 8 table and the class entries. *Searched:* each Ch. 2 class "Hit Dice:" line against the table.
*Found:* agreement for cleric, fighter, magic-user, thief, dwarf, elf, halfling. Druid's "does not apply" is consistent with its 9th-level-cleric entry requirement.
**Disposition: CONFIRMED.**

**Challenge 6 — "The demihuman maximum-hit-point figures are internally consistent."**
*Sought:* arithmetic agreement between Ch. 10's demihuman table and the class entries. *Searched:* Ch. 10 p. 129 table and prose; Ch. 1 p. 12; Ch. 2 elf and dwarf entries.
*Found:* **an unreconciled inconsistency** — see §9.
**Disposition: REJECTED — escalated, not reconciled.**

**Challenge 7 — "The Elf contradiction is a disagreement *between* Chapter 2 and Chapter 10, so each chapter is internally self-consistent and one could be treated as a coherent position."**
*Initial conclusion challenged:* the first version of this packet characterised the Elf problem as a Ch. 1 / Ch. 2 / Ch. 10 disagreement, which implicitly treated each chapter as speaking with one voice.
*Additional passages sought:* every Elf hit-point and maximum-level statement **inside** each chapter, not merely one representative statement per chapter — specifically the Elf class entry's expanded description (as distinct from its stat-block summary), and Chapter 10's "Creating High-Level Player Characters" material (as distinct from its "Maximum Hit Points" table).
*How sought:* the whole Elf class region (Ch. 2, from the Elf stat block to the start of the Halfling entry) was read and scanned for every occurrence of `hit point`, `Hit Dice`, `9th level`, and `10th level`; the whole of Ch. 10's high-level-creation procedure was read through its per-class hit-point list.
*Found:* **the contradiction exists within individual chapters, not only between them.**
- **Chapter 2 contradicts itself:** the Elf stat-block summary states +1 hit point at 10th level, while the Elf expanded "Hit Dice:" description states that **two** additional hit points are gained at 10th level.
- **Chapter 10 contradicts itself:** the Maximum Hit Points (Demihumans) Table's Elf row contributes **+2** (yielding the stated total of 83), while the "Creating High-Level Player Characters" per-class list states the Elf gets **+1 at 10th level**.
**Disposition: REJECTED.** The earlier "one chapter, one position" framing does not survive. **The contradiction remains unresolved** and is now recorded as two separate questions in §9. This matters for Stage B specifically because no chapter may later be cited as uniformly supporting either value.

## 9. ⚠ Internal Source Conflicts — Two Separate Elf Questions

```text
STOP — INTERNAL SOURCE CONFLICT REQUIRES REVIEW
```

RC is internally inconsistent about the Elf in **two distinct ways**. They are related but must not be collapsed into one generic "Elf inconsistency," because they are different questions with different owners and different consequences:

```text
Conflict A —  How high may an Elf advance?
Conflict B —  What HP increment applies once normal Hit Dice stop?
```

A Stage-B agent could, in principle, resolve one without resolving the other. Both are recorded below as complete matrices. **Neither is resolved here.**

### Conflict A — Elf Maximum Level

| RC location | Exact subject | Value stated | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ch. 1, "Maximum Levels and Experience Points" | Maximum level for dwarves and elves, stated together | **12th level** | Ch. 1, p. 12 |
| Ch. 2, Elf class entry — stat-block summary | "Maximum Level:" line | **10** | Ch. 2, Elf entry (TOC pp. 24–26), stat block |

**Shape of Conflict A:** Chapter 1 is the sole outlier at 12. Chapter 2 is internally consistent at 10 across three separate statements, and Chapter 10's prose agrees at 10. **Not resolved here.**

### Conflict B — Elf Fixed HP Gain After Normal Hit Dice

| RC location | Exact subject | Value stated | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ch. 2, Elf class entry — **stat-block summary** | HP gained at 10th level | **+1 hit point** ("10th level, +1 hit point, and Constitution adjustment does not apply") | Ch. 2, Elf entry (TOC pp. 24–26), stat block "Hit Dice:" line |
| Ch. 2, Elf class entry — **expanded "Hit Dice:" description** | HP gained at 10th level | **+2 hit points** ("Two additional hit points are gained at 10th level") | Ch. 2, Elf entry, expanded description |
| Ch. 10, **Maximum Hit Points (Demihumans) Table** | Elf row's post-Hit-Dice contribution, and total | **+2**, giving a stated maximum total of **83** (54 + 27 + 2) | Ch. 10, p. 129, Maximum Hit Points (Demihumans) Table |
| Ch. 10, **"Creating High-Level Player Characters," Step 6** | Elf per-class hit-point line | **+1 at 10th level** ("Elf: 9d6 … plus Constitution bonuses, +1 at 10th level") | Ch. 10, Step 6 per-class list |

**Shape of Conflict B — the point the earlier version of this packet missed:** the disagreement is **not** Chapter 2 versus Chapter 10. **Each chapter contradicts itself.** Chapter 2 says +1 in its stat block and +2 in its expanded prose; Chapter 10 says +2 in its maximum-HP table and +1 in its high-level-creation list. Two locations give +1 and two give +2, split evenly across both chapters.

**Arithmetic note, recorded as an observation and expressly not used to select a value:** the Ch. 10 demihuman table's stated elf total of 83 is arithmetically consistent with +2 and inconsistent with +1 (which would give 82). The dwarf row (72 + 27 + 9 = 108) and halfling row (48 + 24 = 72) are each internally consistent. **This observation does not resolve Conflict B** — internal arithmetic consistency is not an authority rule, and using it to pick a value would be exactly the inference this stage is prohibited from making.

### Why neither conflict is resolved here

RC states no precedence rule among a Chapter 1 summary, a Chapter 2 stat block, a Chapter 2 expanded description, a Chapter 10 table, and a Chapter 10 procedural list. Selecting one would require an inference this stage is not authorized to make. Specifically, **none** of the following was used: counting passages and taking the majority; privileging detailed prose over tables or tables over prose; privileging class entries over general chapters; inferring errata or authorial intent; using arithmetic consistency to select a value; consulting another D&D edition; or proposing a Simulator Ruling (`AGENTS.md` §3, `RULE_CARD_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md` §10, §16).

**The Stage-A conclusion is therefore:** the Rules Cyclopedia is internally inconsistent on both the Elf's maximum level and the Elf's fixed post-Hit-Dice HP gain, and both contradictions require synthesis and/or gap-directed research **after** the Human Evidence Review gate.

### Scope assessment, for the reviewer's triage

- The **core `CHAR-003` procedure is unaffected by either conflict** — Hit Die by class, roll-per-level through Name level, the 1-hit-point floor, the Constitution adjustment and its explicit cut-off are consistent across every location inspected, including the independent Ch. 10 Step 6 restatement.
- **Conflict A** (maximum level) is principally `ADV-002`'s responsibility; it reaches `CHAR-003` only because the cap determines how many Hit Dice are ever rolled.
- **Conflict B** (fixed HP increment) is squarely inside `CHAR-003`'s own responsibility and must be adjudicated before this card's Elf figures can be specified.
- Stage B must not treat any single chapter as uniformly supporting either value in Conflict B, and must not fix elf-specific totals until both are adjudicated. Nothing else in this card is blocked.
