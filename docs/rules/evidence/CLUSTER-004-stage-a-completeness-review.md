## 2. Verdicts

### `EXP-006` — Light & Exploration Resources

```text
PRIMARY-SOURCE COMPLETENESS: FAIL
```

**What was missed.** The packet's two central negative findings are **falsified by the primary
source**:

1. **RC does state the consequence of being in a dungeon without light.** RC p. 150, Chapter 13,
   *Special Character Conditions* → **Blindness**, states that *"a character without infravision
   may find himself in an area of complete darkness"* and assigns that condition **−4 to all
   saving throws, −6 to all attack rolls, +4 to armor class, movement at one-third normal speed
   (measured in feet, not yards), or two-thirds if guided by a sighted character.** The packet's
   §9 Open Question 2 declares this *"the single largest apparent gap in the card"* and states
   *"RC states no consequence for having no light."* That is a source-property claim, and it is
   wrong.
2. **RC does have a light-conditioned table.** The **Encounter Distances Table** (p. 93) is keyed
   on a **Visibility** column — `Very good light` / `Dim light**` / `No light†` for a Dungeon
   setting, driving `4d6×10'` / `2d6×10'` / `1d4×10'` — with footnotes defining
   `** Or full darkness with infravision used` and `† Or very poor visibility`. The packet's §2
   states `LIGHT-BEARING TABLES OR CHECKLISTS FOUND: NONE` and that Chapter 7 contains
   *"No light material."*

Both objects were reachable from instruments the packet says it read: the TOC lists
*"Special Character Conditions ... 150"*; the General Index (p. 302, which §14 says was read
visually) lists **`Blindness ... 150, 154`**; the Index to Tables and Checklists (p. 301, which §2
says was *"read in full, visually"*) lists **`Encounter Distances Table ... 93`**. Two further
named objects in the same index — **`Starvation Table ... 150`** and **`Timetrack Table ... 149`**
— bear directly on the card's *"& Exploration Resources"* half and on its own Open Question 4,
and were likewise not opened.

This is precisely the defect `DEC-0010` §9.1.0 records: a search that returned nothing
light-shaped was promoted into a conclusion about the source. The headline claim that RC governs
light through *"four item descriptions totalling roughly two hundred words, and nothing else"*
cannot stand.

### `ENC-005` — Retreat, Pursuit & Evasion

```text
PRIMARY-SOURCE COMPLETENESS: FAIL
```

**This is a materially lighter failure than EXP-006's, and I want that distinction on the
record.** ENC-005's substantive conclusions are sound. I re-transcribed the Evasion Checklist and
the Evasion Table from the p. 99 page image independently: **every step, every percentage and
every condition-row value in §4.2 and §4.3 is accurate as printed.** Both claimed printed defects
are real (§4 below). The four-of-six dependency claim is correct. Nothing in §4 needs to change.

**What was missed** is coverage, not conclusions:

1. **RC p. 100 column 1 is a governing page for this card and is neither inspected nor correctly
   dispositioned.** It carries the **dropped-goods rule** (*"the DM rolls 1d6 … On a 1-3, the
   monster stops to consume (or retrieve) the proffered goods"*) and the **Regain Bearings**
   definition — both of which the packet quotes in §4.5 and §4.7. Yet §3's object table lists the
   only p. 100 object as *"Ship Evasion Table + naval prose … OCR — **excluded**"*, and §2 states
   *"**p. 100** is the Ship Evasion continuation plus Balancing Encounters. **Neither adds an
   underworld rule.**"* A mechanically significant die roll (1d6, 1–3) is therefore sourced from
   OCR on a page the packet's own coverage record marks as out of scope — a §9.2 visual-gate
   miss and a §9.5 Guardrail B source-property error of the same kind as EXP-006's, at smaller
   scale.
2. **Two printed entry-conditions into this procedure were not followed** (§9.1 audit class G;
   §8 *"entry conditions and exit conditions"*): the **Game Day Checklist step 4b** (p. 91) —
   *"If the characters want to evade or pursue encountered monsters, the DM goes to the 'Evasion
   and Pursuit' section later in this chapter"* — and the **Encounter Checklist step 5c** (p. 93)
   — *"use the pursuit and evasion rules later this chapter to see if the PCs get away."* The
   packet dismisses p. 91 as *"the Encounters introduction (no evasion mechanic)"*; p. 91 also
   carries RC's own definition of the term and the Game Day entry point.
3. **The resolution die is absent from the packet.** RC's worked example continues past where
   §4.3 truncates it: *"The DM rolls a **d100**; on a **01-70**, the PCs have successfully evaded
   the monsters, and on a **71-00**, the monsters successfully pursue the characters."* The
   packet's quotation stops at *"70% chance to evade"* with no ellipsis, and neither §4.2 step 4
   nor §4.3 anywhere states d100 or the roll-under direction. For a card whose whole output is a
   percentage check, this is the governing procedure's single most load-bearing mechanic (§8).
4. **The §9.3 required section does not exist.** §9.3 states that every Stage-A packet *"must
   contain a section titled **Primary-Source Coverage Checklist**."* ENC-005 has none. (Its
   §14.6 refers to *"the completeness claim in §7"*; §7 is *"Findings on scope and boundaries."*)

Because more than one distinct item remains, this is `FAIL` rather than `CONDITIONAL PASS`. I
expect remediation to be a short bounded pass over pp. 91, 93 and 100 plus two structural
sections — not new research.
